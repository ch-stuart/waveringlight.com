ScoreTable: Women’s Lax App

Track every play.

Keep time, score, and stats for women’s lacrosse. Track goals, cards, draws, and 15+ player stats — or just run the clock. Built for NFHS rules.

Features

  • Run just the clock, score, or stats - or run them all at once
  • Manage teams and rosters
  • Configurable periods, timeouts, and overtime
  • Configurable mercy rule with in-game status
  • Goals, assists, shots, and shot percentage
  • Draw player and draw control tracking with per-player win rate analytics
  • Yellow and red card timers and player ejection tracking
  • Automatic woman-up and woman-down goal tracking
  • Goalie saves, goals allowed, and save percentage
  • Full game history with complete stat tables
